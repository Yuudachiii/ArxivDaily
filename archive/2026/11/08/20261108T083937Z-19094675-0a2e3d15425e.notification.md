### PCBRouteAgent: How Close Are LLM Agents to Becoming PCB Routing Engineers?

**中文标题：** PCBRouteAgent：LLM 代理距离成为 PCB 布线工程师还有多远​​？

**摘要：** Automating printed circuit board (PCB) routing requires coordinating board-level connectivity decisions with geometric execution under strict design rules. Although recent agents can interact with native routing tools, reliable routing on complex boards remains challenging. We present PCBRouteAgent, a multimodal PCB routing agent inspired by how human engineers coordinate global strategy with local execution. A Global Routing Planner combines board images and structured electrical and geometric information to specify connection topology, routing order, corridors, and layer transitions. A Detailed Routing Executor realizes these guides through KiCad's native routing engine and performs bounded local recovery. Unresolved failures are returned as structured feedback, enabling targeted guide revisions while preserving accepted routes. We evaluate PCBRouteAgent on 247 synthetic and real-world boards from four PCBWorld-Bench test splits. It achieves clean-pass rates of 100%, 94.9%, 100%, and 40% on D2, D3-A, D3-B, and D3-C, respectively. On the three real-world subsets, PCBRouteAgent outperforms the best evaluated baseline in clean-pass rate by 13.9, 36, and 10 percentage points, respectively.

**摘要翻译：** 自动化印刷电路板 (PCB) 布线需要在严格的设计规则下协调板级连接决策与几何执行。尽管最近的代理可以与本机路由工具交互，但复杂电路板上的可靠路由仍然具有挑战性。我们推出了 PCBRouteAgent，这是一种多模式 PCB 路由代理，其灵感源自人类工程师如何协调全局策略与本地执行。全局布线规划器将电路板图像与结构化电气和几何信息相结合，以指定连接拓扑、布线顺序、走廊和层过渡。详细路由执行器通过 KiCad 的本机路由引擎实现这些指南并执行有界本地恢复。未解决的故障将作为结构化反馈返回，从而实现有针对性的指南修订，同时保留已接受的路线。我们在来自四个 PCBWorld-Bench 测试分组的 247 个合成和真实电路板上评估了 PCBRouteAgent。它在 D2、D3-A、D3-B 和 D3-C 上分别实现了 100%、94.9%、100% 和 40% 的干净通过率。在三个现实世界子集上，PCBRouteAgent 在干净通过率方面分别优于最佳评估基线 13.9、36 和 10 个百分点。

### RouteAlign: Aligning Task Requirements with Model Profiles for LLM Service Selection

**中文标题：** RouteAlign：将任务要求与模型配置文件相结合以进行 LLM 服务选择

**摘要：** LLM service selection requires performance evidence that reflects the demands of the current task. Aggregate scores obscure differences in capability and difficulty, while semantically related examples can involve different combinations of skills. We present RouteAlign, which uses task requirements to structure both the measurement of model performance and the selection of services. Task requirements describe capability demand and difficulty, enabling alignment with corresponding model performance measurements. Historical requirements determine how outcomes contribute to global profiles and local capability estimates; incoming requirements determine how those measurements inform the current decision. A shared scorer integrates the evidence, and a generation-cost preference controls selection among eligible services. Evaluation across diverse tasks and heterogeneous LLM services shows that RouteAlign extends the empirical quality-cost frontier toward higher task quality. Comparisons with open Jev-style decision backends show consistent quality gains, with lower generation cost than their strongest tested configuration. Ablations support the contribution of local performance evidence, while catalog-update experiments demonstrate effective reuse of the fitted scorer with refreshed model measurements.

**摘要翻译：** LLM 服务选择需要反映当前任务需求的绩效证据。总分掩盖了能力和难度的差异，而语义相关的示例可能涉及不同的技能组合。我们提出了 RouteAlign，它使用任务需求来构建模型性能的测量和服务的选择。任务需求描述了能力需求和难度，从而能够与相应的模型性能测量保持一致。历史要求决定了结果如何有助于全球概况和当地能力估计；传入的需求决定了这些测量如何为当前决策提供信息。共享评分器整合证据，发电成本偏好控制合格服务的选择。对不同任务和异构 LLM 服务的评估表明，RouteAlign 将经验质量成本边界扩展到更高的任务质量。与开放式 Jev 式决策后端的比较显示出一致的质量增益，并且生成成本比其最强的测试配置更低。消融支持本地性能证据的贡献，而目录更新实验证明了通过更新的模型测量有效重用拟合的评分器。

**摘要：** This paper presents a novel task of describing human facial expressions of a facial image in natural language, which captures the nuances of facial actions and emotional states beyond traditional emotion categories or facial action units (AUs). To achieve the facial expression captioning model, we propose a three-stage training framework that trains a vision-to-language model using synthesized image-text pairs and the BLIP-2 pre-training techniques. ### Beyond K/V Caches: Cross-Model State Handoff in Hybrid Linear-Attention LLMs

**中文标题：** 超越 K/V 缓存：混合线性注意力 LLM 中的跨模型状态切换

**摘要：** Production deployments often use different models to balance cost and quality through cascading, routing, and switching models during long conversations. Context accumulates across turns. Each switch requires the target to repeat prefill over this context. Existing methods transfer full-attention (FA) K/V caches across models. Hybrid linear-attention models also retain recurrent states (REC) and convolution histories (Conv). The interaction between Conv and REC complicates cross-model state transfer. Conv perturbations can change keys and values in recurrent layers and alter REC updates. Conv histories are overwritten, but changes in REC can still affect later outputs. More accurate REC reconstruction need not improve continuation. Building on this, we propose Hybrid State Transfer (HST) for all three state types. It uses structured REC maps and preserves history order during Conv alignment. We calibrate the adapters using the frozen target's outputs during continuation. FA cache map updates are merged offline. Across five benchmarks, Qwen3.5 4B$\rightarrow$27B and 9B$\rightarrow$27B achieve mean task retentions of 89.88% and 94.11%, respectively. With source caches already available, separate timing on synthetic 16K inputs yields handoff body speedups of $21.66\times$ and $21.91\times$.

**摘要翻译：** 生产部署通常使用不同的模型，通过长时间对话期间的级联、路由和交换模型来平衡成本和质量。上下文会在回合中累积。每个开关都要求目标在此上下文上重复预填充。现有方法跨模型传输全注意力 (FA) K/V 缓存。混合线性注意力模型还保留循环状态（REC）和卷积历史（Conv）。 Conv 和 REC 之间的交互使跨模型状态传输变得复杂。 Conv 扰动可以更改循环层中的键和值并改变 REC 更新。转换历史记录会被覆盖，但 REC 中的更改仍会影响以后的输出。更准确的 REC 重建不需要改善连续性。在此基础上，我们为所有三种状态类型提出混合状态转移（HST）。它使用结构化 REC 映射并在 Conv 对齐期间保留历史顺序。我们在继续过程中使用冻结目标的输出来校准适配器。 FA 缓存映射更新离线合并。在五个基准测试中，Qwen3.5 4B$\rightarrow$27B 和 9B$\rightarrow$27B 的平均任务保留率分别为 89.88% 和 94.11%。由于源缓存已经可用，合成 16K 输入上的单独计时可产生 $21.66\times$ 和 $21.91\times$ 的切换主体加速。

### Linguistic boundaries emerge from pressure-constrained processing dynamics

**中文标题：** 语言边界源于压力受限的处理动态

**摘要：** Human cognition organizes continuous experience into bounded units while maintaining continuity across them. Linguistic elements are also organized into bounded structural units such as words and phrases, with sentence marking the final bound of a grammatical construction process. How do such boundaries emerge? We propose boundaries emerge from interacting demands to maintain unresolved dependencies, integrate completed relations, and update semantic context, with structural completion allowing but not determining closure. First, in a generative simulation without predefined boundary positions or constituent labels, these demands produced sentence-like segment-length distributions and distinct characteristic timescales, with structural completion allowing but not determining closure. Near boundaries, unresolved dependency load declined, integration cost peaked, and accumulated pressure reset. Then, we tested whether same demands predicted human listeners' boundary choices during comprehension. Listeners were more likely to mark boundaries with fewer unresolved dependencies, higher integration cost and semantic mismatch, and elevated preceding memory load, beyond syntactic and prosodic cues. Third, in naturalistic self-paced reading, sentence-final slowing coincided with declining memory load and increasing integration cost and semantic mismatch, broadly consistent with the simulation. Processing same narratives with a large language model revealed increased local intrinsic dimensionality and straighter hidden-state trajectories at sentence endings, followed by larger representational updates. Boundary explained additional variation in reading time and hidden-state geometry beyond processing demands, but considerably less in next-token surprisal. Together, these findings suggest linguistic boundaries emerge as discrete manifestations of transitions in continuous processing dynamics.

**摘要翻译：** 人类认知将连续的经验组织成有界的单元，同时保持它们之间的连续性。语言元素也被组织成有界的结构单元，例如单词和短语，句子标志着语法构建过程的最终界限。这样的界限是如何出现的？我们建议边界从交互需求中出现，以维护未解决的依赖关系，集成已完成的关系，并更新语义上下文，结构完成允许但不确定封闭。首先，在没有预定义边界位置或成分标签的生成模拟中，这些需求产生了类似句子的段长度分布和独特的特征时间尺度，结构完成允许但不确定闭合。在边界附近，未解决的依赖负载下降，集成成本达到顶峰，累积的压力重置。然后，我们测试了相同的要求是否可以预测人类听众在理解过程中的边界选择。听众更有可能通过更少的未解决的依赖关系、更高的集成成本和语义不匹配以及更高的先前记忆负载来标记边界，超越句法和韵律线索。第三，在自然主义的自定进度阅读中，句子最终减慢与记忆负载下降、集成成本增加和语义不匹配同时发生，与模拟大致一致。使用大型语言模型处理相同的叙述揭示了局部内在维度的增加和句子结尾处更直的隐藏状态轨迹，随后是更大的表征更新。边界解释了超出处理需求的读取时间和隐藏状态几何的额外变化，但下一个令牌的惊喜要少得多。总之，这些发现表明语言边界是作为连续处理动态转变的离散表现而出现的。

### DictLLM: Post-training Compression of Large Language Model with Dictionary Kernels

**中文标题：** DictLLM：使用字典内核对大型语言模型进行训练后压缩

**摘要：** Efficient deployment of large language models (LLMs) on mobile devices requires balancing performance with compute and memory constraints. Post-training compression is an effective approach to managing this trade-off, while requiring much less computation than in-training counterparts. We propose DictLLM, a dictionary-based compression scheme in which weight matrices within grouped layers are decomposed into a shared dictionary and layer-specific sparse coefficients, directly obtained from a pre-trained model without further training. Specifically, we cast DictLLM as a four-step post-training optimization. First, we obtain shared dictionaries via singular value decomposition (SVD) of input-scaled weight matrices, amortizing rank across grouped layers; second, we sparsify the layer-specific coefficients to the target compression ratio using a dictionary-aware Hessian; and third, we refine the dictionaries given the resulting sparse coefficients. Finally, we aggressively quantize both components using factor-specific Hessian information: input covariance for the dictionaries and the dictionary-aware Hessian for the coefficients. The resulting representation reduces memory footprint by storing a single shared dictionary per layer group, along with sparse layer-specific coefficients. On language modeling and zero-shot reasoning benchmarks, DictLLM consistently outperforms existing post-training low-rank compression methods and pushes the Pareto frontier between model size and performance. DictLLM compresses the original LLaMA-7B model by 5.1x with only a 2.9-point increase in WikiText-2 perplexity, achieving a 3.6x higher compression ratio than the state-of-the-art low-rank method.

**摘要翻译：** 在移动设备上高效部署大型语言模型 (LLM) 需要平衡性能与计算和内存限制。训练后压缩是管理这种权衡的有效方法，同时比训练中的压缩所需的计算量要少得多。我们提出了 DictLLM，一种基于字典的压缩方案，其中分组层内的权重矩阵被分解为共享字典和特定于层的稀疏系数，直接从预训练的模型中获得，无需进一步训练。具体来说，我们将 DictLLM 视为四步训练后优化。首先，我们通过输入缩放权重矩阵的奇异值分解（SVD）获得共享字典，在分组层之间摊销排名；其次，我们使用字典感知的 Hessian 矩阵将特定于层的系数稀疏化为目标压缩比；第三，我们根据所得的稀疏系数来完善字典。最后，我们使用特定于因子的 Hessian 信息积极量化这两个分量：字典的输入协方差和系数的字典感知 Hessian 矩阵。由此产生的表示通过为每个层组存储一个共享字典以及稀疏的特定于层的系数来减少内存占用。在语言建模和零样本推理基准测试中，DictLLM 始终优于现有的训练后低秩压缩方法，并推动模型大小和性能之间的帕累托前沿。 DictLLM 将原始 LLaMA-7B 模型压缩了 5.1 倍，而 WikiText-2 困惑度仅增加了 2.9 点，实现了比最先进的低秩方法高 3.6 倍的压缩比。

### Spatiotemporal Dynamics-Aware Industrial Anomaly Detection and Reasoning

**中文标题：** 时空动态感知工业异常检测与推理

**摘要：** Industrial Anomaly Detection (IAD) is a long-established problem that aims to detect defects in industrial products during manufacturing for quality control. Most existing studies focus on static visual patterns as the basis for detection. However, in real manufacturing environments, many anomalies are dynamics-based, such as irregular movements that cannot be perceived from static cues alone. We observe that even recent Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM)-based approaches fall short in identifying such dynamic anomalies due to limited temporal modeling capability. To address this, we introduce MUSTARD: MUltimodal SpatioTemporal Anomaly Reasoning and Detection, a framework that equips MLLMs with fine-grained spatiotemporal dynamics awareness for detecting and reasoning about dynamics-based defects. MUSTARD introduces optical-flow-guided keyframe selection and augmented modalities to capture critical motion irregularities through local motion patterns and global dynamic evolution. Its normal pattern-based reasoning pipeline requires only a few normal reference samples as the detection basis, avoiding any model updates. This enables efficient adaptation across various products and provides high decision interpretability. MUSTARD is the first MLLM-based framework for dynamics-based IAD. Experiments demonstrate its superior performance in detecting spatiotemporal anomalies and providing plausible explanations. The code will be made publicly available.

**摘要翻译：** 工业异常检测（IAD）是一个长期存在的问题，旨在检测制造过程中工业产品的缺陷以进行质量控制。大多数现有研究侧重于静态视觉模式作为检测的基础。然而，在真实的制造环境中，许多异常都是基于动态的，例如仅通过静态线索无法感知的不规则运动。我们观察到，由于时间建模能力有限，即使是最近基于多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的方法也无法识别此类动态异常。为了解决这个问题，我们引入了 MUSTARD：多模态时空异常推理和检测，这是一个为 MLLM 配备细粒度时空动态感知的框架，用于检测和推理基于动态的缺陷。 MUSTARD 引入了光流引导关键帧选择和增强模式，通过局部运动模式和全局动态演化来捕获关键运动不规则性。其正常的基于模式的推理管道仅需要少量正常的参考样本作为检测基础，避免任何模型更新。这使得能够有效地适应各种产品并提供高度的决策可解释性。 MUSTARD 是第一个基于 MLLM 的基于动力学的 IAD 框架。实验证明其在检测时空异常和提供合理解释方面具有卓越的性能。该代码将公开。

### Can an LLM Actually Reason and Program about Neural Network's Weights or is it just memorization on steroids? Exploring "Weight-Space Programming"

**中文标题：** 法学硕士实际上可以对神经网络的权重进行推理和编程，还是只是加强记忆？探索“权重空间规划”

**摘要：** Large language models (LLMs) have recently been reported to read and write the raw weights of neural networks directly, a capability termed weight-space programming. We ask whether the reading side of this capability reflects reasoning about weight flows or a combination of local arithmetic at the network's edges and memorized priors over public datasets. We test Claude Opus 5.5 on small convolutional networks of about 125,000 parameters trained on five image datasets, providing every weight as plain text with no tools, no forward passes, and no dataset or class names. The model performs accurate local calculations: batch-normalization statistics lead it to infer about three optimizer steps on networks stopped after three updates, and output-layer sign patterns recover the vehicle/animal split of CIFAR-10. The sessions report reading the first and last layers closely and sampling the middle, and several claims built on partial tracing fail verification, although we did not run a direct forward-pass test. Semantic judgments lean on familiar datasets: the model names CIFAR-10 for CIFAR-10 networks but also for all three fast-food networks, relabels KMNIST as MNIST, reads a 99.57%-accurate SAT-6 network as CIFAR-10 animal photos, and calls "frog" the output favored by a feature that fires most on trees. Permuting CIFAR-10's labels preserves the output split at the new indices, but every class pair the model interprets is then wrong, indicating reliance on remembered label order. These are exploratory results from mostly single runs. We conclude that zero-shot weight reading by this model is better described as local arithmetic plus dataset priors than as weight-flow tracing, and we provide a protocol for auditing future weight-space claims.

**摘要翻译：** 最近有报道称，大型语言模型（LLM）可以直接读取和写入神经网络的原始权重，这种能力称为权重空间编程。我们询问此功能的读取方面是否反映了对权重流的推理或网络边缘的本地算术与公共数据集上记忆的先验的组合。我们在五个图像数据集上训练的约 125,000 个参数的小型卷积网络上测试 Claude Opus 5.5，以纯文本形式提供每个权重，无需工具、无需前向传递，也无需数据集或类名称。该模型执行准确的本地计算：批量归一化统计数据使其能够推断出在三次更新后停止的网络上的大约三个优化器步骤，并且输出层符号模式恢复了 CIFAR-10 的车辆/动物分裂。会话报告仔细读取第一层和最后一层并对中间进行采样，尽管我们没有运行直接的前向测试，但基于部分跟踪构建的一些声明未能通过验证。语义判断依赖于熟悉的数据集：模型将 CIFAR-10 网络命名为 CIFAR-10，同时也将所有三个快餐网络命名为 CIFAR-10，将 KMNIST 重新标记为 MNIST，将 99.57% 准确度的 SAT-6 网络读取为 CIFAR-10 动物照片，并将最喜欢在​​树上触发的特征称为“青蛙”输出。排列 CIFAR-10 的标签会保留新索引处的输出分割，但模型解释的每个类对都是错误的，表明依赖于记住的标签顺序。这些是大多数单次运行的探索性结果。我们得出的结论是，该模型的零样本权重读取更好地描述为局部算术加数据集先验，而不是权重流跟踪，并且我们提供了用于审核未来权重空间声明的协议。

### KeHoSu: Keyed Homoglyph Substitution for Authenticated Text Provenance over Copy-Paste Channels

**中文标题：** KeHoSu：通过复制粘贴通道进行键控同形文字替换以验证文本来源

**摘要：** Nowadays, large language models produce text at scale, and much of it is redistributed across platforms. A moderator who finds a suspicious comment, post or article may want to know which platform generated it and for which account. In this paper we study attribution over the channel through which such text actually travels, copy-paste, which discards document metadata but keeps Unicode code points. We present KeHoSu, which turns the confusable characters of homoglyph attacks into a keyed attribution channel: the variant chosen for each carrier letter encodes one bit of an identifier made of a platform field, a user field and a truncated authentication tag. Moreover, given that reshared text is often quoted in fragments, and the placement therefore keeps no positional state, we show that KeHoSu is invariant to sentence reordering up to a constant boundary layer and depends on a partial quotation only through the retained length. We characterize the code-point channel by its capacity, removability and detectability, and provide an achievable region that relates the three. We evaluate KeHoSu over a wide range of use cases and show that the mark survived copy-paste across 18 common platforms, attributing the majority of all excerpts to the correct account at 178 letters (about two sentences) on LLM-generated stories and 167 letters (about one sentence) on standard English prose.

**摘要翻译：** 如今，大型语言模型可以大规模生成文本，其中大部分文本可以跨平台重新分发。发现可疑评论、帖子或文章的版主可能想知道是哪个平台生成的以及哪个帐户生成的。在本文中，我们研究了此类文本实际传播的渠道（复制粘贴）的归因，该渠道会丢弃文档元数据但保留 Unicode 代码点。我们提出了 KeHoSu，它将同形文字攻击的易混淆字符转变为密钥归因通道：为每个载体字母选择的变体对由平台字段、用户字段和截断的身份验证标签组成的标识符的一位进行编码。此外，考虑到重新共享的文本经常在片段中被引用，因此放置不保持位置状态，我们表明 KeHoSu 对于句子重新排序在恒定边界层上是不变的，并且仅通过保留的长度依赖于部分引用。我们通过其容量、可移除性和可检测性来描述码点通道的特征，并提供将这三者联系起来的可实现区域。我们在广泛的用例中评估了 KeHoSu，结果表明该标记在 18 个常见平台上的复制粘贴中幸存下来，将所有摘录中的大部分归因于正确的描述，其中法学硕士生成的故事有 178 个字母（约两个句子），标准英语散文有 167 个字母（约一个句子）。

### Automatic keyphrase extraction and annotation: modern theoretical approaches and practical solutions for text and speech

**中文标题：** 自动关键短语提取和注释：文本和语音的现代理论方法和实用解决方案

**摘要：** The exponential growth of textual and audiovisual information has made the task of automatic keyphrase extraction (KE) increasingly significant. This article provides a comprehensive analysis of contemporary theoretical approaches and practical solutions for KE across both text and speech modalities. The primary contribution of this work is its systematic synthesis of these often-disparate research strands into a unified analytical framework, highlighting the evolution of the field from statistical methods towards large language models (LLMs) and end-to-end speech processing. We examine the stages of KE, the characteristics of keyphrases in written and spoken language, and terminological nuances. Various methods for automatic KE are discussed and analyzed in detail: statistical, hybrid, machine learning-based, and structural. The review dedicates substantial attention to emerging paradigms, including keyphrase generation using LLMs, and provides a detailed overview of methodologies and challenges in automatic corpus annotation. Furthermore, we specifically analyze current directions and inherent difficulties in KE for spoken language, comparing transcript based and end-to-end acoustic approaches. This synthesis leads us to conclude that the field is moving towards a more integrated, context-aware paradigm. Future progress will depend on addressing key challenges such as data scarcity for low-resource languages, effective multimodal fusion, and the nuanced evaluation of generative KE systems.

**摘要翻译：** 文本和视听信息的指数级增长使得自动关键短语提取（KE）的任务变得越来越重要。本文全面分析了文本和语音模式中 KE 的当代理论方法和实际解决方案。这项工作的主要贡献是将这些通常不同的研究分支系统地综合成一个统一的分析框架，突出了该领域从统计方法到大型语言模型（LLM）和端到端语音处理的演变。我们研究了 KE 的阶段、书面和口语中关键短语的特征以及术语的细微差别。详细讨论和分析了自动 KE 的各种方法：统计方法、混合方法、基于机器学习的方法和结构方法。该评论重点关注新兴范例，包括使用法学硕士生成关键短语，并详细概述了自动语料库注释的方法和挑战。此外，我们还专门分析了口语 KE 的当前方向和固有困难，比较了基于转录的声学方法和端到端声学方法。这种综合使我们得出结论，该领域正在朝着更加集成、情境感知的范式发展。未来的进展将取决于解决关键挑战，例如低资源语言的数据稀缺、有效的多模态融合以及生成 KE 系统的细致评估。
