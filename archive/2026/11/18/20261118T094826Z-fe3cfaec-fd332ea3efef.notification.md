### Ambient LLMs: Data-Efficient Supervised Fine-tuning via Temperature Matching

**中文标题：** 环境法学硕士：通过温度匹配进行数据高效的监督微调

**摘要：** Collecting large-scale, diverse, and high-quality post-training datasets for autoregressive language models is challenging. Consequently, Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) is typically applied to datasets significantly smaller than those used for pre-training, which leads to overfitting and the catastrophic forgetting of the tails of the data distribution. To prevent this collapse, we introduce Ambient SFT, a principled framework for leveraging imperfect predictions to augment the fine-tuning set. In our method, a "frozen" version of the pre-trained model serves as a noisy teacher that the fine-tuned model must match at a coarse level. In particular, we require that the high-temperature versions of the conditional distributions produced by the post-training model should match the high-temperature versions of the pre-training ones. The temperature hyperparameter controls the level of trust in the pre-trained model, with the limit $T\to \infty$ reverting back to regular SFT and $T=1$ to knowledge distillation. We demonstrate that our approach outperforms standard SFT across domain adaptation, instruction-tuning, and reasoning, leading for up to $21\%$ average relative improvement to standard SFT.

**摘要翻译：** 为自回归语言模型收集大规模、多样化且高质量的训练后数据集具有挑战性。因此，监督微调（SFT）通常应用于比预训练使用的数据集小得多的数据集，这会导致过度拟合和数据分布尾部的灾难性遗忘。为了防止这种崩溃，我们引入了 Ambient SFT，这是一个利用不完美预测来增强微调集的原则框架。在我们的方法中，预训练模型的“冻结”版本充当噪声老师，微调模型必须在粗略水平上匹配。特别是，我们要求训练后模型产生的条件分布的高温版本应与训练前模型的高温版本相匹配。温度超参数控制预训练模型的信任级别，限制 $T\to \infty$ 恢复为常规 SFT，$T=1$ 恢复为知识蒸馏。我们证明，我们的方法在领域适应、指令调整和推理方面优于标准 SFT，从而使标准 SFT 的平均相对改进高达 $21\%$。

### Do LLM Agents Still Need to Be Told How to Self-Improve?

**中文标题：** LLM代理人还需要被告知如何自我提升吗？

**摘要：** LLM agents increasingly drive their own learning. Self-improvement methods delegate various components of model optimization to the models themselves. As models become more capable, this motivates a basic question: do these increasingly capable models still need us to specify how they should self-improve? In this paper we study an autonomous paradigm in which, given basic primitives, resources, and a general goal for self-improvement, agents construct their own self-learning algorithm for context-based optimization. Using the setting of gameplay as a testbed, a frozen LLM agent is given a compute budget and tools for collecting experience and updating text documents, and allowed to freely experiment without an externally imposed loop or access to real-world data. We find that sufficiently capable models do self-improve under these conditions, demonstrating strategies that align with classic cognitive frameworks for self-regulated learning and constructs from machine learning literature on self-generated curricula. Furthermore, we study the extent to which the learned text artifacts transfer to external benchmarks, observing improvement across different game horizons, mechanics, and 3D navigation.

**摘要翻译：** LLM 代理人越来越多地推动自己的学习。自我改进方法将模型优化的各个组成部分委托给模型本身。随着模型的能力越来越强，这就引发了一个基本问题：这些能力越来越强的模型是否仍然需要我们指定它们应该如何自我改进？在本文中，我们研究了一种自主范式，在该范式中，给定基本原语、资源和自我改进的总体目标，代理构建自己的自学习算法以进行基于上下文的优化。使用游戏设置作为测试平台，冻结的 LLM 代理将获得计算预算和用于收集经验和更新文本文档的工具，并允许自由实验，而无需外部强加的循环或访问现实世界的数据。我们发现，在这些条件下，足够有能力的模型确实可以自我改进，展示了与自我调节学习的经典认知框架相一致的策略，以及根据关于自我生成课程的机器学习文献构建的策略。此外，我们还研究了学习到的文本工件转移到外部基准的程度，观察不同游戏视野、机制和 3D 导航的改进。

### What Does a Bias Head Measure? Non-Identifiability in Head-Level Bias Attribution

**中文标题：** 偏置头测量什么？高层偏见归因中的不可识别性

**摘要：** Methods that localise social bias in large language models to individual attention heads and remove it by ablation are validated by their effect on a fairness metric, never by evidence that the statistic selecting the heads measures bias. We audit this on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. The head map correlates at $ρ= -0.950$ and $-0.768$ with a label-blind response-length map, apparently identifying a surface confound. An intervention that breaks the length-label coupling rejects this reading: with the prompt held fixed, response length explains 1% of the statistic's variance. What does explain it is prompt identity, at 64% of variance, in an ordering that is not monotonic in length. Within any fixed prompt the fairness label, response length and the presence of a protected-group token are collinear at $φ= 0.95$, partly by definition of the labelling rule, so no observational analysis can attribute the map to any of them, and no method in this family performs an experiment that could. The substantive claims fail independently: bias selectivity is not late-layer-concentrated on either model under either reference reading, and no head set we tested reduces the unfairness rate when ablated. Its complement, scored on the opposite label, reliably destroys refusal behaviour ($p = 2.7\times10^{-5}$), demonstrating that the pipeline detects effects at this scale.

**摘要翻译：** 将大型语言模型中的社会偏见定位到个体注意力头并通过消融消除它的方法是通过它们对公平性度量的影响来验证的，而不是通过选择头的统计数据衡量偏见的证据来验证。我们在 Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct 和 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 上对此进行审核。头部图在 $ρ= -0.950$ 和 $-0.768$ 处与标签盲响应长度图相关，显然识别了表面混淆。打破长度-标签耦合的干预措施会拒绝这种解读：在提示保持固定的情况下，响应长度解释了统计方差的 1%。能解释这一点的是，方差为 64%，并且长度顺序不单调。在任何固定提示中，公平标签、响​​应长度和受保护组标记的存在在 $φ= 0.95$ 处共线，部分是由标签规则的定义决定的，因此没有观察分析可以将映射归因于其中任何一个，并且该系列中没有方法执行可以做到这一点的实验。实质性主张独立失败：在任一参考读数下，偏差选择性都不是后期层集中在任一模型上，并且我们测试的任何耳机都不会降低消融时的不公平率。它的补充，在相反的标签上得分，可靠地破坏了拒绝行为（$p = 2.7\times10^{-5}$），表明管道检测到了这种规模的影响。

### MoE--LoRA Methods for Multilingual Adaptation: A Systematic Empirical Study

**中文标题：** MoE--LoRA 多语言适应方法：系统实证研究

**摘要：** Routed low-rank adapters offer a parameter-efficient means to improve the performance of large language models (LLMs) across tasks. Hence, it is assumed that they can also be used to improve the support of low-resource languages in LLMs, which currently serve English far better than the thousands of other languages spoken worldwide. However, this assumption has never been verified empirically under a shared multilingual evaluation protocol. We address exactly this gap by comparing eight low-rank adaptation (LoRA) methods on a frozen base model using 200 unique languages in more than 9{,}000 evaluation settings. Our results suggest that a reduced-scale search can identify viable configurations of LoRA methods but does not reliably predict their relative performance, as the top-ranking configuration is never best at scale. LoRA reliably improves language-model fit on translation data, consistently degrades reading comprehension, and benefits low-resource languages most. These findings suggest that LoRA methods should be regarded as targeted tools rather than general-purpose means for improving the multilingual capabilities of LLMs.

**摘要翻译：** 路由低等级适配器提供了一种参数高效的方法来提高大型语言模型（LLM）跨任务的性能。因此，人们认为它们也可以用来改善法学硕士对资源匮乏语言的支持，目前法学硕士对英语的支持远远好于全世界使用的数千种其他语言。然而，这一假设从未在共享的多语言评估协议下得到实证验证。我们通过在冻结的基础模型上使用 200 种独特语言在 9{,}000 多个评估设置中比较八种低秩适应 (LoRA) 方法来准确解决这一差距。我们的结果表明，缩小规模的搜索可以识别 LoRA 方法的可行配置，但不能可靠地预测其相对性能，因为排名靠前的配置从来都不是规模上最好的。 LoRA 可靠地改善了翻译数据的语言模型拟合度，持续降低了阅读理解能力，并且对资源匮乏的语言最有利。这些发现表明，LoRA 方法应被视为提高法学硕士多语言能力的有针对性的工具，而不是通用手段。

### Learning Stratified Policies for Inference-Time Objectives

**中文标题：** 学习推理时间目标的分层策略

**摘要：** Inference-time scaling improves the performance of large language models by sampling multiple responses and evaluating their collective utility. However, many existing approaches obtain these samples by repeatedly sampling from a single policy. In this work, we study if the sample budget at inference time can instead be allocated across a population of policies. We introduce stratified policies, where each of $k$ learned policies contributes one response to a $k$-sample inference-time objective. We realize this policy population using separate LoRA adapters on a shared language model backbone and optimize it for pass@$k$. We theoretically motivate the approach by analyzing the linear setting; a population of LoRAs can achieve zero best-of-$k$ approximation error in settings where every single LoRA has strictly positive prediction error. Empirically, we demonstrate that stratified policies consistently improve pass@$k$ over a single-policy baseline across multiple reasoning tasks. Overall, our results highlight how distributing samples across complementary policies can be a more effective use of sample budget than repeated i.i.d. sampling from a single policy at inference time.

**摘要翻译：** 推理时间缩放通过对多个响应进行采样并评估其集体效用来提高大型语言模型的性能。然而，许多现有方法通过从单个策略中重复采样来获取这些样本。在这项工作中，我们研究是否可以将推理时的样本预算分配到政策群体中。我们引入了分层策略，其中每个 $k$ 学习策略都对 $k$ 样本推理时间目标做出一个响应。我们在共享语言模型骨干上使用单独的 LoRA 适配器来实现这一策略群体，并针对 pass@$k$ 对其进行优化。我们通过分析线性设置从理论上推动了该方法；在每个 LoRA 都具有严格正预测误差的设置中，LoRA 群体可以实现零 best-of-k$ 近似误差。根据经验，我们证明分层策略在多个推理任务中比单一策略基线持续提高 pass@$k$。总体而言，我们的结果强调了如何在互补政策中分配样本比重复独立同分布更有效地利用样本预算。在推理时从单个策略中采样。

### VIVID: Beyond Static Multi-Image Reasoning with Localized Visual Differences and Textual Entity Dynamics in Video MLLMs

**中文标题：** VIVID：超越静态多图像推理，具有视频 MLLM 中的局部视觉差异和文本实体动态

**摘要：** Video question answering under tight frame budgets requires preserving localized motion and event order, yet current video multimodal large language models (MLLMs) reduce comprehension to static multi-image reasoning by discarding unselected frames and encoding keyframes independently: (1) Spatially, whole-frame pooling dilutes small moving targets into static backgrounds; (2) Temporally, dropping intermediate frames or averaging tokens erases inter-frame motion, confusing direction, speed, and order. We propose VIVID (Visual Inter-frame Variations and Individual Dynamics), which moves beyond static snapshots like human vision: keyframes capture core states while inter-frame Patch Diffs preserve neighboring changes within $K_f$ keyframes. Autonomous Delta-Driven Keyframe Planning (ADKP) selects anchors from a $3\text{ fps}$ pool and feeds surrounding patch differences into Dual-Road Change Memory (DRCM), forming Dynamic Tokens at zero extra anchor tokens alongside a camera-stabilized Active Motion Region of Interest (ROI) and chronological Entity State Chains. Adding $0.22\%$ trainable parameters to a frozen $27\text{B}$ backbone, VIVID-Small ($50$ examples, $\sim 1.5$ GPU hours) beats all eleven baselines in overall accuracy across $8,083$ questions and trails only VIVID-Large ($3,600$ examples), which leads on MotionBench, Video-MME, and TempCompass.

**摘要翻译：** 在紧张的帧预算下进行视频问答需要保留局部运动和事件顺序，但当前的视频多模态大语言模型（MLLM）通过丢弃未选择的帧并独立编码关键帧来减少对静态多图像推理的理解：（1）在空间上，全帧池化将小型移动目标稀释为静态背景； (2) 暂时地，丢弃中间帧或平均令牌会消除帧间运动、混乱的方向、速度和顺序。我们提出了 VIVID（视觉帧间变化和个体动态），它超越了人类视觉等静态快照：关键帧捕获核心状态，而帧间补丁差异保留 $K_f$ 关键帧内的相邻变化。自主增量驱动关键帧规划 (ADKP) 从 $3\text{ fps}$ 池中选择锚点，并将周围的补丁差异馈送到双路更改内存 (DRCM) 中，在零额外锚点标记处形成动态标记，以及相机稳定的主动运动感兴趣区域 (ROI) 和按时间顺序排列的实体状态链。将 $0.22\%$ 可训练参数添加到冻结的 $27\text{B}$ 主干中，VIVID-Small（$50$ 示例，$\sim 1.5$ GPU 小时）在 $8,083$ 问题的总体准确度上击败了所有 11 个基线，并且仅落后于 VIVID-Large（$3,600$ 示例），后者领先于 MotionBench、Video-MME 和 TempCompass。

### Mind the Gap: A Deployed Multi-Agent System for Documentation Maintenance

**中文标题：** 注意差距：用于文档维护的已部署多代理系统

**摘要：** The "AI revolution" has enabled teams across the technology industry to deliver software features at unprecedented velocity. While code evolves faster than ever, customer-facing documentation often struggles to stay up to speed. As users increasingly rely on LLM-based agents that consume documentation as their primary interface, the impact of this documentation gap worsens: stale information propagates and is treated as authoritative. Signals for identifying where documentation has fallen behind already exist in abundance, both explicit (e.g., resolved support tickets revealing what documentation lacks) and implicit (e.g., code-documentation discrepancies detectable from automated loops). We deploy a multi-agent system, built around a triage agent we call "TriAgent," that continuously extracts these signals and repairs documentation drift. Over 43 days of shadow-mode operation, our system triaged 499 tickets and surfaced 92 gaps in the documentation. Using a panel of LLM judges from independent model families, 81.1% of TriAgent's verifiable assertions were correct (245 of the 302 assertions the human resolution addresses; majority of three judges); on length-comparable pairs the panel preferred TriAgent's response in 70.5% of pairs and the human engineer's in 23.2% (the rest had no judge majority). Importantly, agent- and human-sourced replies tended to be complementary, with the human often surfacing insights that were either buried within the existing documentation or missing from it entirely; our system turns each such insight into a documentation gap and, after human review, a fix. Repairing a gap measurably improved the agent's later answer: when the agent re-answered the same ticket after the repair, blinded judges preferred the post-repair answer over its own pre-repair answer 71.4% of the time (n=35). A blinded human audit confirms strong alignment on self-improvement.

**摘要翻译：** “人工智能革命”使整个技术行业的团队能够以前所未有的速度交付软件功能。虽然代码的发展速度比以往任何时候都快，但面向客户的文档往往难以跟上步伐。随着用户越来越依赖基于 LLM 的代理，这些代理将文档作为主要界面，这种文档差距的影响日益恶化：过时的信息会传播并被视为权威信息。用于识别文档落后之处的信号已经大量存在，既有显式的（例如，已解决的支持票证揭示了文档缺乏的内容），也有隐式的（例如，可从自动循环中检测到代码文档差异）。我们部署了一个多代理系统，围绕我们称为“TriAgent”的分类代理构建，该系统不断提取这些信号并修复文档偏差。经过 43 天的影子模式运行，我们的系统对 499 个故障单进行了分类，并发现了文档中的 92 个空白。使用来自独立模型家族的法学硕士法官小组，TriAgent 的可验证断言中有 81.1% 是正确的（人类决议解决的 302 个断言中的 245 个；三名法官中的大多数）；在长度相当的配对中，70.5% 的配对小组更喜欢 TriAgent 的反应，23.2% 的人更喜欢人类工程师的反应（其余的没有多数法官）。重要的是，代理和人工的回复往往是互补的，人类经常会提出一些见解，这些见解要么隐藏在现有文档中，要么完全缺失；我们的系统将每一个这样的见解转化为文档空白，并在人工审查后进行修复。修复漏洞显着改善了客服人员后来的回答：当客服人员在修复后重新回答同一张票时，盲法法官在 71.4% 的情况下更喜欢修复后的答案，而不是修复前的答案 (n=35)。盲法人工审核证实了自我改进的强烈一致性。

### RSI-Prime: Training Models to Train Models

**中文标题：** RSI-Prime：训练模型来训练模型

**摘要：** Enabling large language models (LLMs) to autonomously conduct training and improve target models is an important foundation for recursive self-improvement (RSI). As LLMs expand their role from being trained to conducting training, their model-training capability warrants dedicated study and systematic development. To this end, we construct RSI-Prime, a dataset for improving model-training capability, comprising 350 complete experimental trajectories across seven task categories. To help LLMs learn to make high-quality experimental decisions from training practice, we perform fine-grained decision identification and quality annotation, then apply decision-level loss masking to exclude supervision on low-quality behavior. Experiments with GLM-5.3 on PostTrainBench, a benchmark for autonomous post-training, show that training on RSI-Prime yields a 31.95% relative improvement in overall performance over the original GLM-5.3, highlighting a path toward future RSI systems in which models autonomously sustain their own advancement.

**摘要翻译：** 使大型语言模型（LLM）能够自主进行训练并改进目标模型是递归自我改进（RSI）的重要基础。随着法学硕士将其角色从接受培训扩大到进行培训，他们的模型培训能力值得专门研究和系统开发。为此，我们构建了 RSI-Prime，这是一个用于提高模型训练能力的数据集，包含跨七个任务类别的 350 个完整的实验轨迹。为了帮助法学硕士从培训实践中学习做出高质量的实验决策，我们执行细粒度的决策识别和质量注释，然后应用决策级损失屏蔽来排除对低质量行为的监督。在 PostTrainBench（自主后训练基准）上使用 GLM-5.3 进行的实验表明，与原始 GLM-5.3 相比，在 RSI-Prime 上进行训练的整体性能相对提高了 31.95%，这凸显了通向未来 RSI 系统的道路，其中模型可以自主维持自身的进步。

### Get Hint-Augmented GRPO Done Right Through Activation Steering

**中文标题：** 通过激活指导正确完成提示增强的 GRPO

**摘要：** GRPO has become the dominant reinforcement learning method for eliciting reasoning capability in LLMs using verifiable rewards. However, it suffers from a fundamental failure mode: when a training question exceeds the base model's current capability, all generated responses fail and receive zero reward, yielding no gradient signal and stalling further capability expansion. An emerging class of GRPO extensions, termed Hint-Augmented GRPO (HA-GRPO), adds a "hint" to the context to reduce the difficulty of the question, thereby producing a usable gradient signal. Nevertheless, existing HA-GRPO methods share a key issue: no hint is available at inference time to append to the question, creating a training-inference context mismatch that leads to inconsistent performance gains. In this work, we reinterpret the function of hinting -- not as reducing question difficulty, but as steering model behavior -- and propose ASH (Activation Steering for Hinting), a novel HA-GRPO method built on this view. During training, the model is presented with both hinted and plain questions, while a trainable steering vector is optimized to widen the margins between the model's generation probabilities for responses to the hinted version relative to the plain version, thereby capturing the question-agnostic component of the behavioral shift induced by the hint. During inference, the trained steering vector is directly injected into the model's activation, reproducing the hint-conditioned behavior without requiring any hint in the context. Experiments with four LLMs (Qwen3-1.7B/4B-Base, Llama-3.2-1B/3B-Instruct) show that ASH consistently improves pass@$k$ on competition-level mathematics benchmarks (AMC, OlympiadBench, AIME24, AIME25) and out-of-distribution benchmarks (Minerva, GPQA-Diamond). Notably, it improves average pass@8 of Qwen3-1.7B-Base and Qwen3-4B-Base by \textbf{2.60}\% and \textbf{2.21}\%.

**摘要翻译：** GRPO 已成为使用可验证奖励来激发法学硕士推理能力的主要强化学习方法。然而，它遇到了一个基本的失败模式：当训练问题超过基础模型的当前能力时，所有生成的响应都会失败并获得零奖励，不会产生梯度信号并阻碍进一步的能力扩展。一类新兴的 GRPO 扩展被称为提示增强 GRPO (HA-GRPO)，它向上下文添加“提示”以降低问题的难度，从而产生可用的梯度信号。然而，现有的 HA-GRPO 方法有一个关键问题：在推理时没有可用的提示来附加到问题中，从而造成训练-推理上下文不匹配，从而导致性能增益不一致。在这项工作中，我们重新解释了提示的功能——不是降低问题难度，而是引导模型行为——并提出了 ASH (Activation Steering for Hinting)，一种基于此观点的新颖的 HA-GRPO 方法。在训练过程中，向模型提出暗示问题和普通问题，同时优化可训练的引导向量，以扩大模型相对于普通版本对暗示版本的响应的生成概率之间的裕度，从而捕获由提示引起的行为转变的问题不可知部分。在推理过程中，经过训练的引导向量被直接注入到模型的激活中，再现提示条件行为，而不需要上下文中的任何提示。对四个法学硕士（Qwen3-1.7B/4B-Base、Llama-3.2-1B/3B-Instruct）进行的实验表明，ASH 在竞赛级数学基准（AMC、OlympiadBench、AIME24、AIME25）和分布外基准（Minerva、GPQA-Diamond）上持续提高 pass@$k$。值得注意的是，它将 Qwen3-1.7B-Base 和 Qwen3-4B-Base 的平均 pass@8 提高了 \textbf{2.60}\% 和 \textbf{2.21}\%。

### Dynamic Task Allocation with Dialogue

**中文标题：** 对话动态任务分配

**摘要：** Effective multi-agent collaboration requires agents to adapt task allocation as goals and responsibilities evolve. Prior task-allocation frameworks often assume static goals, full observability, or rely on large language models (LLMs) without explicit, verifiable beliefs about task allocation or intent. These assumptions limit their applicability to real-world collaboration, where agents often lack full information about each other's actions, progress, or local environment. To support dynamic task allocation under partial observability, we introduce a communication-augmented Bayesian task-allocation framework. Our agent uses dialogue to reduce uncertainty about the environment and collaborators, while keeping decisions grounded in explicit probabilistic beliefs. We evaluate our framework in an Overcooked domain extended with dynamically arriving tasks under both full and partial object-space observability. Our communication agent consistently outperforms a vanilla LLM agent in both settings. Additionally, our framework performs comparably to a non-communicating task-allocation baseline under full observability, but substantially outperforms it under partial observability as task complexity increases by an average speed up of 0.585 and completes on average 18 timesteps faster. These results indicate that our agent is more efficient in dynamic task allocation than baselines in partially observable environments, and that communication is important as available information decreases.

**摘要翻译：** 有效的多智能体协作需要智能体随着目标和职责的变化而调整任务分配。先前的任务分配框架通常假设静态目标、完全可观察性，或依赖大型语言模型 (LLM)，而没有关于任务分配或意图的明确、可验证的信念。这些假设限制了它们在现实世界协作中的适用性，其中代理通常缺乏有关彼此的行为、进度或本地环境的完整信息。为了支持部分可观察性下的动态任务分配，我们引入了通信增强贝叶斯任务分配框架。我们的代理使用对话来减少环境和合作者的不确定性，同时保持决策基于明确的概率信念。我们在一个 Overcooked 域中评估我们的框架，该域在完整和部分对象空间可观察性下扩展了动态到达的任务。我们的沟通代理在这两种设置中始终优于普通的 LLM 代理。此外，我们的框架在完全可观察性下的性能与非通信任务分配基线相当，但在部分可观察性下的性能明显优于非通信任务分配基线，因为任务复杂性平均加速了 0.585，完成时间平均快了 18 个时间步长。这些结果表明，我们的代理在动态任务分配方面比部分可观察环境中的基线更有效，并且随着可用信息的减少，沟通变得很重要。
