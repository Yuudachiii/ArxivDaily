### TideCache: Efficient Hybrid LLM Serving with Transparent Cache Management and Layer-Wise Streaming

**中文标题：** TideCache：具有透明缓存管理和分层流传输的高效混合 LLM 服务

**摘要：** Hybrid architectures have become mainstream among open-weight language models. Their global-attention and bounded-state layers maintain heterogeneous caches whose demands vary with context length and concurrency. We present TideCache, a serving system on CPU--GPU superchips with three components for allocating GPU memory among caches and managing their residency across GPU and host memory. The Transparent Cache Pool redistributes physical pages among cache components behind fixed virtual addresses, preserving cache layouts and backend interfaces. The Cache Streamer uses computation intervals provided by bounded-state layers to prefetch the next global-attention layer's prefix into GPU slots shared across global-attention layers, reducing GPU residency while overlapping layer-wise transfers with computation. Generated KV remains resident. The Request Scheduler selects resident or streamed execution and triggers capacity adjustments according to request size and queue state. Together, they let HBM released by streaming accommodate other cache components and additional requests. Implemented in SGLang and evaluated on GB300 and GH200 across seven model/platform configurations, TideCache completes up to 2.04x as many agent turns, reduces overall P50 per-token latency by 20--59%, and improves agent P50 TTFT by 1.40--3.05x compared with SGLang with HiCache.

**摘要翻译：** 混合架构已成为开放权重语言模型的主流。它们的全局注意力和有界状态层维护异构缓存，其需求随着上下文长度和并发性而变化。我们推出了 TideCache，这是一个基于 CPU-GPU 超级芯片的服务系统，具有三个组件，用于在缓存之间分配 GPU 内存并管理它们在 GPU 和主机内存之间的驻留。透明缓存池在固定虚拟地址后面的缓存组件之间重新分配物理页，保留缓存布局和后端接口。 Cache Streamer 使用有界状态层提供的计算间隔将下一个全局注意层的前缀预取到全局注意层之间共享的 GPU 插槽中，从而减少 GPU 驻留，同时将分层传输与计算重叠。生成的 KV 仍然驻留。请求调度器根据请求大小和队列状态选择驻留或流式执行并触发容量调整。它们共同让通过流式发布的 HBM 容纳其他缓存组件和额外的请求。与采用 HiCache 的 SGLang 相比，TideCache 在 SGLang 中实施，并在 GB300 和 GH200 上跨七种模型/平台配置进行评估，完成的代理轮次最多为 2.04 倍，每个令牌的总体 P50 延迟降低了 20--59%，并将代理 P50 TTFT 提高了 1.40--3.05 倍。

### SoK: Assessing the Evolution of LLM Capability in Software Vulnerability Research

**中文标题：** SoK：评估软件漏洞研究中法学硕士能力的演变

**摘要：** LLM-based agents are becoming increasingly capable at discovering, validating and patching vulnerabilities in real software, and vendors and researchers report that this is already changing how vulnerabilities are found and fixed. These claims, however, are scattered across papers, vendor reports and disclosures and are rarely comparable. We introduce a framework that rates these capabilities on an autonomy scale modeled on the levels of driving automation, and qualifies each claim by its correctness, its availability and the strength of its evidence. We use this framework to systematize the published evidence on LLM capability in vulnerability research. We find that agents discovered, validated, and patched vulnerabilities in real-world software without human intervention before the restricted frontier models of 2026, and that open-weight models subsequently attained comparable autonomy for discovery and validation. We then measure the consequences of evolving LLM security capabilities in a decade of public vulnerability records, advisories and exploitation data. In 2026 the number of disclosed vulnerabilities more than doubled, with an increasing share rated critical or high, and severe bugs that had survived a decade or more in widely used code were reported several times more often than before. Yet the time between the stages of a vulnerability's life, from report to fix and from disclosure to attack, has not shifted noticeably so far. We grade the mitigations proposed in the literature with an explicit rubric and draw guidance for each group of stakeholders from the best-supported ones.

**摘要翻译：** 基于 LLM 的代理在发现、验证和修补真实软件中的漏洞方面的能力越来越强，供应商和研究人员报告说，这已经改变了漏洞的发现和修复方式。然而，这些说法分散在论文、供应商报告和披露中，很少具有可比性。我们引入了一个框架，该框架根据驾驶自动化水平建模的自主规模对这些功能进行评级，并根据每项主张的正确性、可用性和证据强度来验证其资格。我们使用这个框架来系统化已发表的关于漏洞研究中法学硕士能力的证据。我们发现，在 2026 年的受限前沿模型之前，代理无需人工干预即可发现、验证和修补现实世界软件中的漏洞，并且开放权重模型随后获得了类似的发现和验证自主权。然后，我们在十年的公共漏洞记录、建议和利用数据中衡量不断发展的 LLM 安全能力的后果。 2026 年，披露的漏洞数量增加了一倍以上，严重或高级别漏洞的比例不断增加，广泛使用的代码中存在十年或更长时间的严重错误的报告频率比以前高出数倍。然而，漏洞生命周期的各个阶段（从报告到修复、从披露到攻击）之间的时间到目前为止还没有明显变化。我们使用明确的标准对文献中提出的缓解措施进行分级，并从最受支持的利益相关者中为每组利益相关者提供指导。

### Verify the Verifier: Specification-Grounded LLM Cross-Checking for Bugs That Escape UVM Scoreboards

**中文标题：** 验证验证者：基于规范的 LLM 交叉检查是否存在 UVM 记分板的错误

**摘要：** A passing UVM regression shows the design agrees with the reference model on what the scoreboard checks, not that it meets the specification. Bugs that pass every test yet violate the specification, termed scoreboard escapes, survive mature testbenches. We present Verify the Verifier, to our knowledge the first framework whose LLM-based Anomaly Cross-Checker examines a passing regression's traces against the specification. Of 564 bugs inserted into four OpenTitan IPs, 68 are candidate scoreboard escapes: non-equivalent, exercised by the tests, yet passing. The framework confirmed 41 as scoreboard escapes with assertions failing on the buggy design and proven on the original.

**摘要翻译：** 通过 UVM 回归表明设计与记分板检查的参考模型一致，而不是表明它符合规范。通过所有测试但违反规范的错误（称为记分板逃逸）可以在成熟的测试平台中生存下来。我们提出了Verify the Verifier，据我们所知，这是第一个框架，其基于LLM的异常交叉检查器根据规范检查通过回归的痕迹。在四个 OpenTitan IP 中插入的 564 个错误中，有 68 个是候选记分板逃逸：非等效，已通过测试，但已通过。该框架确认了 41 为记分板逃逸，断言在错误设计上失败并在原始版本中得到了验证。

### Replication of Agentic Security Events: Audit and Investigation (ROGUE-AI)

**中文标题：** 代理安全事件的复制：审计和调查 (ROGUE-AI)

**摘要：** Agents based on large language models (LLMs) can now delete files and send payments on behalf of their users. News media reports routinely describe failures of these agents (e.g., Replit's coding agent deleting a production database), but a report often only says what went wrong and rarely (in)validates the incident or finds its root cause. Incident catalogs collect these reports, and benchmarks record whether agents fail, but neither replicates an incident nor tests the cause its report might identify. In this paper, we present ROGUE-AI (Replication of Agentic Security Events: Audit and Investigation), a multi-agent framework that closes the above gap and rebuilds each reported incident offline together with a matched control that lacks only the blamed cause, and runs agents on both. Our results suggest that ROGUE-AI is able to reproduce the reported harm in 89% of its 108 attempted replications. Agents built on five current GPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek models made the reported mistake in 18% to 33% of runs, against at most 1% without the blamed cause. Of the 72 incidents with real-world harm, 62 had no underlying attacker, and in these, agents did what their task asked for but missed an ordinary hazard, such as a database shared with the live site. Replications without an attacker caused more harm than replications of prompt injection for all five models. Defenses against prompt injection look for injected instructions, but these replications contain none. Further, a warning after each tool output that names the hazard and its consequence lowered harm for all five models, whereas a note of the same length that does not mention the hazard left most of the harm in place. Built from real incidents, the ROGUE-AI replications form a reusable audit framework that vendors can rerun before each new release.

**摘要翻译：** 基于大型语言模型 (LLM) 的代理现在可以代表用户删除文件并发送付款。新闻媒体报道通常会描述这些代理的故障（例如，Replit 的编码代理删除生产数据库），但报告通常只说明出了什么问题，很少验证事件或找到其根本原因。事件目录收集这些报告，基准测试记录代理是否失败，但既不复制事件，也不测试其报告可能识别的原因。在本文中，我们提出了 ROGUE-AI（代理安全事件的复制：审计和调查），这是一个多代理框架，可以弥补上述差距，并离线重建每个报告的事件以及仅缺少归咎原因的匹配控制，并在两者上运行代理。我们的结果表明，ROGUE-AI 能够在 108 次尝试复制中的 89% 重现所报告的危害。基于当前五种 GPT、Claude、Gemini 和 DeepSeek 模型构建的代理在 18% 到 33% 的运行中犯了所报告的错误，而没有归咎原因的情况下最多只有 1%。在 72 起造成现实损害的事件中，有 62 起没有潜在的攻击者，在这些事件中，特工执行了任务要求的操作，但错过了一个普通的危险，例如与实时站点共享的数据库。对于所有五个模型，没有攻击者的复制比立即注入的复制造成的危害更大。针对提示注入的防御会寻找注入的指令，但这些复制不包含任何指令。此外，每个工具输出后的警告都指出了危险及其后果，降低了所有五个模型的危害，而相同长度的注释没有提及危险，从而保留了大部分危害。 ROGUE-AI 复制基于真实事件构建，形成了一个可重用的审计框架，供应商可以在每个新版本之前重新运行该框架。

### The Geometry of Lies: Knowledge and Persona Directions for Detecting Deception in Large Language Models

**中文标题：** 谎言的几何：在大型语言模型中检测欺骗的知识和角色方向

**摘要：** Linear probes are the cheapest LLM lie detectors but often fail to transfer across kinds of lie. Label-free directions from honest and deceptive role-play are untested at 70B scale and on lies about knowledge, where a lie and an honest denial can be identical. We widen a published persona bank with roles that deny knowledge they have, plus honest counterparts. At each model's middle layer the resulting persona direction ranks lies across Liars' Bench better than single-dataset probes on three of its four models; it fails on Qwen, even when Qwen stays in role far more often. On Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct it beats directions from a random persona bank, but its gain over the published direction is within the range of random widenings. On lies about knowledge, persona directions mainly track a reply's wording: where lies and honest denials share their words, our three main contrasts stay inside the random-direction band on Llama and Qwen and reach only its edge on Gemma, though on Mistral one clears it. A knowledge direction contrasts questions the model can answer with ones nobody can, each followed by the same scripted denial. It separates lies from identical honest denials on two models and narrowly on a third, but not on the fourth; its other results partly depend on the benchmark's wording. On the Gender-Secret fine-tune it clears the band by a few hundredths on Llama and fails on Mistral. The sum of the two directions ranks lies better than a supervised probe at the same depth on all four models (on one, both near chance) but is level with the persona direction on Llama's average and behind that probe on held-out lies. More varied role-play questions do not help the persona direction on Liars' Bench.

**摘要翻译：** 线性探针是最便宜的法学硕士测谎仪，但通常无法跨类型的谎言进行转移。来自诚实和欺骗性角色扮演的无标签指示未经 70B 规模和关于知识的谎言的测试，其中谎言和诚实的否认可以是相同的。我们扩大了一个公开的角色库，其中包括否认自己拥有知识的角色，以及诚实的同行。在每个模型的中间层，所得到的人物角色方向排名在撒谎者工作台上的排名优于对其四个模型中的三个进行单数据集探测；它在 Qwen 上失败了，即使 Qwen 更频繁地担任角色。在 Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct 上，它击败了随机角色库的方向，但其相对于已发布方向的增益在随机扩大的范围内。关于知识的谎言，角色方向主要跟踪回复的措辞：在谎言和诚实的否认分享他们的话语的地方，我们的三个主要对比停留在 Llama 和 Qwen 上的随机方向带内，并且仅到达 Gemma 的边缘，尽管在 Mistral 上一个清除了它。知识方向将模型可以回答的问题与无人能回答的问题进行对比，每个问题都遵循相同的脚本否认。它区分了两个模型上的谎言和相同的诚实否认，以及第三个模型上的谎言和相同的诚实否认，但第四个模型上则不然；其其他结果部分取决于基准的措辞。在 Gender-Secret 微调中，它在 Llama 上将频段清除了百分之几，而在 Mistral 上则失败了。两个方向排名的总和在所有四个模型上都比相同深度的监督探测更好（其中一个，两者都接近机会），但与 Llama 的平均值上的人物角色方向持平，并且在保留谎言上落后于该探测。更多不同的角色扮演问题无助于在说谎者席上进行角色定位。

### LLM GerryMandering: Separating Interactions from Additive Effects in LLM Audits

**中文标题：** LLM GerryMandering：将 LLM 审计中的相互作用与加性效应分开

**摘要：** Intersectional bias arises when a model treats people who hold several marginalised identities at once differently from what their separate identities would predict. Auditing such intersectional subgroups remains a challenge: their number grows quadratically, and there is no agreed baseline for what independent attributes would predict. Without such a baseline, many existing audits flag a subgroup when it exceeds each attribute alone, a condition that attributes acting independently already satisfy. We test this standard criterion on 150 synthetic datasets built with no interaction. With the shuffle null, it flags 66.1 false subgroups per dataset on average, and at least one in every dataset. When we plant interactions of known size into these datasets, a repaired null lets the criterion recover only 32% of them. We introduce ADDIS (Additive Intersectional Screen), which fits an additive model over all attributes, scores each subgroup by its Additive Residual Distance (ARD) from that model's prediction, and tests it against a Freedman-Lane null. On our synthetic benchmark, ADDIS flags 0.09 false subgroups per dataset and recovers 84% of the same planted interactions. We then build IndiPersona, 10,000 synthetic Indian personas with six identity attributes, and audit 13 open language models. The standard criterion flags 7.1% of the 738 tested subgroups on average; ADDIS flags 3.0%. Gender identity crossed with sexual orientation is the largest pairing in every model, and counterfactual personas confirm it and additionally reveal a caste x class interaction in all but one model that the observational test lacked the data to detect. Much of what audits report as intersectional bias can thus arise from independent single-attribute effects alone, yet a small core of interactions survives.

**摘要翻译：** 当模型以不同的方式对待同时拥有多种边缘化身份的人时，就会出现交叉偏见。审计此类交叉子组仍然是一个挑战：它们的数量呈二次方增长，并且对于独立属性的预测没有商定的基线。如果没有这样的基线，许多现有的审核会在子组超出每个属性单独时标记一个子组，这是独立作用的属性已经满足的条件。我们在 150 个没有交互的情况下构建的合成数据集上测试了这个标准。在 shuffle null 的情况下，它平均每个数据集标记 66.1 个错误子组，并且每个数据集中至少有一个。当我们将已知大小的相互作用植入这些数据集中时，修复的空值使标准只能恢复其中的 32%。我们引入了 ADDIS（加法交叉筛选），它适合所有属性的加法模型，根据模型预测的加法残差 (ARD) 对每个子组进行评分，并针对 Freedman-Lane 零值进行测试。在我们的综合基准测试中，ADDIS 为每个数据集标记了 0.09 个错误子组，并恢复了 84% 的相同植入交互作用。然后，我们构建了 IndiPersona，即 10,000 个具有 6 个身份属性的合成印度角色，并审核 13 个开放语言模型。标准标准平均标记了 738 个测试亚组中的 7.1%； ADDIS 标记为 3.0%。性别认同与性取向的交叉是每个模型中最大的配对，反事实角色证实了这一点，并另外揭示了除了观察测试缺乏数据检测的数据之外的所有模型中的种姓与阶级相互作用。因此，审计报告中的大部分交叉偏见可能仅由独立的单属性效应引起，但仍有一小部分核心的相互作用存在。

### Cost-Aware Agentic Feature Optimization System for Fleet-Scale Ranking Models

**中文标题：** 适用于舰队规模排名模型的成本感知代理特征优化系统

**摘要：** Large Web platforms operate large fleets of ranking models, each selecting features from a shared pool of tens of thousands. We present an end-to-end agentic system that optimizes features across the fleet under resource constraints shared across models and those set per model, such as feature pipeline cost and serving throughput (QPS), respectively. In this system, LLM agents propose hypotheses, and deterministic tools execute experiments and verify them. For each model, an LLM generates and shortlists cross features from a combinatorial candidate space, and all features are then ranked by several importance methods. Feature blending mixes these rankings into a single selection, using Pareto fronts across tasks for multi-task models. An LLM Researcher agent then tunes the feature selection in 10--25 asynchronous trials under resource caps, and a trimming step keeps the pipeline cost within budget. Across production models, the system improves normalized entropy by 0.31% on average within each model's budget, and the trimming step cuts pipeline cost by 2.3% with no regression beyond measurement noise. The optimized models were launched, and in online A/B tests they improved a primary online metric by 0.20%.

**摘要翻译：** 大型网络平台运行着大量的排名模型，每个模型都从数以万计的共享池中选择特征。我们提出了一个端到端的代理系统，该系统可以在跨模型共享的资源约束和每个模型设置的资源约束下优化整个机队的功能，例如功能管道成本和服务吞吐量（QPS）。在这个系统中，LLM代理提出假设，确定性工具执行实验并验证它们。对于每个模型，法学硕士都会从组合候选空间中生成交叉特征并将其列入候选名单，然后通过几种重要性方法对所有特征进行排名。特征混合将这些排名混合成一个选择，使用跨任务的帕累托前沿来构建多任务模型。然后，LLM 研究人员代理会在资源上限下的 10--25 次异步试验中调整功能选择，并通过调整步骤将管道成本保持在预算范围内。在整个生产模型中，系统在每个模型的预算内将归一化熵平均提高了 0.31%，并且修剪步骤将管道成本降低了 2.3%，并且没有超出测量噪声的回归。优化后的模型已上线，在线 A/B 测试中，主要在线指标提高了 0.20%。

### AutoMaster: Autonomous Post-Training by Making the Same Model a Better Teacher

**中文标题：** AutoMaster：自主后期训练，让同一个模型成为更好的老师

**摘要：** Task success alone does not specify which behaviors post-training should reinforce. Even successful teacher trajectories can contain unsupported assumptions, unproductive repetition, or superficial verification. We propose Skill-FT (Skill-Guided Fine-Tuning), which uses natural-language skills to suppress undesirable habits and elicit underrepresented effective behaviors during teacher rollouts, then fine-tunes a target model on the resulting trajectories. We introduce AutoMaster, an autonomous post-training system in which an LLM agent develops these interventions through trajectory analysis, skill design, data selection, and feedback from rollouts and target-model evaluation. Across Terminal, Search, and software engineering tasks with 4B and 9B target models, AutoMaster improves mean performance over Vanilla SFT on successful unguided teacher trajectories by 1.42-8.99 percentage points, with the teacher, task pool, training set size, and SFT recipe held fixed. Together with the ablations and trajectory analyses, these results suggest that behavioral shaping can make a fixed model a more effective teacher. We further find that retaining skill instructions during SFT yields higher mean performance than removing them across all six settings, despite their absence at evaluation and identical supervised outputs. These findings suggest that preserving skill context as part of a training demonstration can support better behavioral internalization.

**摘要翻译：** 任务成功本身并不能指定培训后应该强化哪些行为。即使成功的教师轨迹也可能包含不受支持的假设、无效的重复或肤浅的验证。我们提出技能-FT（技能引导微调），它使用自然语言技能来抑制不良习惯并在教师推出期间引发未被充分代表的有效行为，然后根据产生的轨迹微调目标模型。我们引入了 AutoMaster，这是一个自主的培训后系统，其中法学硕士代理通过轨迹分析、技能设计、数据选择以及推出和目标模型评估的反馈来开发这些干预措施。在具有 4B 和 9B 目标模型的终端、搜索和软件工程任务中，在教师、任务池、训练集大小和 SFT 配方保持固定的情况下，AutoMaster 在成功的无引导教师轨迹上比 Vanilla SFT 的平均性能提高了 1.42-8.99 个百分点。结合消融和轨迹分析，这些结果表明行为塑造可以使固定模型成为更有效的教师。我们进一步发现，在 SFT 期间保留技能指令比在所有六种设置中删除它们产生更高的平均性能，尽管它们在评估时不存在且监督输出相同。这些发现表明，保留技能背景作为培训演示的一部分可以支持更好的行为内化。

### Multimodal Error Detection in Chest X-ray Reports: A Large Language Model Reading Image Evidence

**中文标题：** 胸部 X 光报告中的多模态错误检测：读取图像证据的大型语言模型

**摘要：** Rad-Linter checks chest X-ray reports against image-derived evidence to flag potential errors for review. A pretrained image classifier supplies scores for nine pathologies, and a 1.5B-parameter language model, fine-tuned with LoRA on 1,816 examples, checks their consistency with the report. We compare it with a keyword-and-negation rule baseline given the same reports and scores. Evaluation uses controlled report-score pairings from OpenI, with consistency labels derived from automatic report labels and score thresholds. Each counterfactual quad crosses two real reports with two real score vectors from other patients, creating two consistent and two inconsistent pairs. Every report and score vector occurs in both conditions, so either input alone permits only 50% accuracy. On 131 quads, Rad-Linter achieves 98.5% pair accuracy versus 80.5% for the rule baseline, and classifies all four pairs correctly in 94.4% of quads versus 56.5%. The checkers perform similarly on omission items, including unmentioned and negated findings; larger differences occur on the remaining item types, which require interpreting how findings are described. The same language model without fine-tuning remains near 50% accuracy. On a second set of 181 quads that also includes reports on which the automatic labelers disagree, pair accuracy is 93.4% versus 75.3%. These controlled results show that, given the same image-derived scores, a trained language model detects report-score inconsistencies more accurately than the tested rule baseline, with the advantage concentrated in interpreting how findings are worded.

**摘要翻译：** Rad-Linter 根据图像衍生证据检查胸部 X 光报告，以标记潜在错误以供审查。预训练的图像分类器提供 9 种病理学的分数，并且使用 LoRA 对 1,816 个示例进行微调的 1.5B 参数语言模型检查它们与报告的一致性。我们将其与给出相同报告和分数的关键字和否定规则基线进行比较。评估使用来自 OpenI 的受控报告分数配对，以及源自自动报告标签和分数阈值的一致性标签。每个反事实四边形将两个真实报告与来自其他患者的两个真实评分向量交叉，创建两个一致和两个不一致的对。每个报告和分数向量都会在这两种情况下出现，因此单独的任一输入仅允许 50% 的准确度。在 131 个四边形上，Rad-Linter 的配对准确度为 98.5%，而规则基线为 80.5%，并且在四边形中正确分类所有四对的比例为 94.4%，而规则基线为 56.5%。检查员对遗漏项目的表现类似，包括未提及和否定的发现；其余项目类型存在较大差异，需要解释如何描述结果。未经微调的相同语言模型仍保持接近 50% 的准确率。在第二组 181 个四边形中，也包括自动贴标机不同意的报告，配对准确度分别为 93.4% 和 75.3%。这些对照结果表明，在相同的图像衍生分数的情况下，训练有素的语言模型比测试的规则基线更准确地检测报告分数的不一致，其优势集中在解释结果的措辞方式。

### A new algorithmic approach for finding flat minima: critically damped Polyak stepsizes

**中文标题：** 寻找平坦最小值的新算法方法：临界阻尼 Polyak 步长

**摘要：** We present a new adaptive stepsize rule that biases gradient descent toward flatter minima in both theory and practice, and establish the first uniform-in-time guarantees for both sharpness and loss reduction for a broad class of locally quadratic objectives. Our critically damped Polyak (CDP) stepsizes multiply the classical Polyak rule by $4$ and a mild damping factor. The factor $4$ is critical in that it produces equioscillations around the manifold of minimizers $\mathcal{M}$ to leading order. Damping provably drives convergence to $\mathcal{M}$. CDP thus operates at the edge of stability by adapting its stepsizes to local curvature. Once the loss is small, its projected iterates follow gradient descent on $\log{λ_1(\nabla^2 \mathcal{L})}$ along $\mathcal{M}$ to leading order, paralleling the flatness bias of normalized gradient descent (Arora et al., 2022). Unlike normalized gradient descent, CDP step lengths shrink with the distance to $\mathcal{M}$, enabling convergence to an interpolating solution that locally minimizes sharpness at finite learning rate. We establish our theory for a broad class of non-negative losses with locally quadratic Morse-Bott structure near $\mathcal{M}$, capturing regular interpolation regimes in smooth overparameterized neural networks. Empirically, CDP stepsizes produce a flattening effect similar to that of Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM, Foret et al., 2021), reducing sharpness even in settings beyond our theory, including nonlinear teacher-student networks and fine-tuning of pre-trained LLMs.

**摘要翻译：** 我们提出了一种新的自适应步长规则，该规则在理论和实践中使梯度下降偏向更平坦的最小值，并为广泛的局部二次目标的锐度和损失减少建立了第一个统一的时间保证。我们的临界阻尼 Polyak (CDP) 步长将经典 Polyak 规则乘以 4 美元和温和的阻尼系数。因子 $4$ 至关重要，因为它会在最小化流形 $\mathcal{M}$ 周围产生等振荡，以达到领先顺序。阻尼可证明推动收敛到 $\mathcal{M}$。因此，CDP 通过调整其步长以适应局部曲率，在稳定性边缘运行。一旦损失很小，它的预测迭代就会遵循 $\log{λ_1(\nabla^2 \mathcal{L})}$ 上的梯度下降，沿着 $\mathcal{M}$ 到前导顺序，与归一化梯度下降的平坦度偏差平行（Arora 等人，2022）。与归一化梯度下降不同，CDP 步长随着到 $\mathcal{M}$ 的距离而缩小，从而能够收敛到在有限学习率下局部最小化锐度的插值解决方案。我们建立了针对一类广泛的非负损失的理论，其局部二次 Morse-Bott 结构接近 $\mathcal{M}$，捕获平滑超参数化神经网络中的规则插值机制。根据经验，CDP 步长会产生类似于锐度感知最小化（SAM，Foret 等人，2021）的平坦化效果，即使在超出我们理论的设置中也能降低锐度，包括非线性师生网络和预训练 LLM 的微调。

**来源：** [arXiv 2611.26475v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26475v1)

**初筛路径：** llm_training、llm_core
