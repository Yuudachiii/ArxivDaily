### Diverse Alternative Reasoning Traces for Hard Math Problems

**中文标题：** 困难数学问题的多种替代推理轨迹

**摘要：** Post-training large language models (LLMs) for mathematical reasoning often relies on accepted traces whose final answers pass a verifier. For hard math problems, however, such traces can be rare under independent Monte Carlo (MC) sampling. To address this, we propose Diverse Alternative Reasoning Traces (DART), a trace-mining method that starts from a rejected trace, decomposes it into reasoning steps, and ranks them by position-weighted uncertainty. It then generates deliberately distinct alternative candidates for highly ranked steps using reference-answer guidance and a meta-thought before completing the resulting traces. On greedy-unsolved DeepMath-103K problems, DART recovers 37% more problems than MC sampling at a matched number of generated traces. Its estimated generation cost per recovered problem is nearly identical, while its cost per accepted trace is 30% lower. Post-training Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on traces mined by DART achieves the highest point estimate in five of six evaluations across MATH-500 and AIME benchmarks. These results demonstrate that revisiting rejected traces with DART can expand problem coverage and provide effective post-training data.

**摘要翻译：** 用于数学推理的大型语言模型 (LLM) 的后训练通常依赖于已接受的痕迹，其最终答案可以通过验证者。然而，对于困难的数学问题，在独立蒙特卡罗 (MC) 采样下，此类痕迹可能很少见。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了多样化替代推理轨迹（DART），这是一种轨迹挖掘方法，从被拒绝的轨迹开始，将其分解为推理步骤，并按位置加权不确定性对它们进行排名。然后，在完成生成的跟踪之前，它会使用参考答案指导和元思想为排名较高的步骤故意生成不同的候选候选。对于贪婪未解决的 DeepMath-103K 问题，DART 在匹配数量的生成迹线下比 MC 采样多恢复 37% 的问题。每个已恢复问题的估计生成成本几乎相同，而每个已接受跟踪的成本要低 30%。训练后的 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 对 DART 挖掘的痕迹在 MATH-500 和 AIME 基准的六项评估中的五项中实现了最高点估计。这些结果表明，使用 DART 重新访问被拒绝的跟踪可以扩大问题覆盖范围并提供有效的训练后数据。

### Agentic LongLive: Asynchronous Agentic Memory for Long Video Generation

**中文标题：** Agentic LongLive：用于长视频生成的异步代理内存

**摘要：** Real-time autoregressive video generators stream minutes of video and accept new prompts on the fly, but struggle to keep people, objects, and places consistent over a long interactive story. Agent systems provide such memory for offline multi-shot generation, yet they read the whole script in advance, plan each shot before generating it, and regenerate failed shots, which a live stream does not allow. We present Agentic LongLive, an asynchronous agentic memory for long video generation. A memory agent on separate GPUs reads only the prompts revealed so far, never the generated video, and keeps a text state and evolving images for every person, object, and scene. From this entity memory it composes a global image and a per-shot memory image, which an auxiliary GPU prefills into the generator's attention sink before the next shot starts, and writes entity cards for the prompts that name them, so the video never waits for memory and video errors are not written back. A small language model, trained with reinforcement learning and an image reward calibrated using downstream video outcomes, decides what to remember before the next prompt is known. On LongStory-Online, a new benchmark of 25 stories (857 shots) revealed one shot at a time, Agentic LongLive improves persistent-state accuracy of LongLive-2.0, its base generator, by 26.3 points, protagonist identity by 44.9, and revisited-place consistency by 40.4, at about 1% extra generation time on H100. The full system streams faster than real time on one 8xB200 node with the generator in BF16 (34.3 FPS), keeps image quality stable over 14-minute stories, and improves identity on two MiniMax-H3 generators, one of which, LongLive-H3, also runs in real time with it. To our knowledge, it is the first real-time video generator with text and image memory kept by an LLM agent.

**摘要翻译：** 实时自回归视频生成器可以传输几分钟的视频并即时接受新的提示，但很难在长互动故事中保持人物、物体和地点的一致。代理系统为离线多镜头生成提供这样的内存，但它们提前读取整个脚本，在生成每个镜头之前对其进行规划，并重新生成失败的镜头，这是直播流所不允许的。我们推出了 Agentic LongLive，一种用于长视频生成的异步代理内存。单独 GPU 上的内存代理仅读取迄今为止显示的提示，而不会读取生成的视频，并保留每个人、物体和场景的文本状态和不断变化的图像。它从这个实体内存中组成一个全局图像和一个每次镜头内存图像，辅助 GPU 在下一个镜头开始之前将其预填充到生成器的注意力池中，并为命名它们的提示写入实体卡，因此视频永远不会等待内存，并且视频错误也不会被写回。一个小型语言模型，经过强化学习训练和使用下游视频结果校准的图像奖励，决定在知道下一个提示之前要记住什么。在 LongStory-Online 上，25 个故事（857 个镜头）的新基准一次显示一个镜头，Agentic LongLive 将其基本生成器 LongLive-2.0 的持久状态准确度提高了 26.3 分，主角身份提高了 44.9 分，重访地点一致性提高了 40.4 分，在 H100 上额外生成时间约为 1%。整个系统在一个 8xB200 节点上使用 BF16 (34.3 FPS) 生成器进行流传输，速度比实时更快，在 14 分钟的故事中保持图像质量稳定，并提高了两个 MiniMax-H3 生成器的身份，其中之一 LongLive-H3 也与其实时运行。据我们所知，它是第一个由 LLM 代理保存文本和图像内存的实时视频生成器。

### ProbFaS: Probabilistic Video Frame Selection for MLLMs via Multi-Fidelity Calibration

**中文标题：** ProbFaS：通过多保真度校准进行 MLLM 的概率视频帧选择

**摘要：** Processing long videos with multimodal large language models (MLLMs) is prohibitively expensive, making informative frame selection essential for accurate video understanding. The default uniform sampling often misses task-relevant keyframes, while recent training-free selectors instead pick frames by how well each one matches the question under an image-text alignment (ITA) score. However, we show that ITA signals correspond poorly with the MLLM's true needs: their rankings correlate weakly with downstream accuracy and are even negatively correlated on over 40% of questions, whereas the MLLM's internal yes/no judgment yields a much stronger signal. We present ProbFaS, a training- and label-free probabilistic pipeline that fuses both signals for key frame selection. ProbFaS treats dense ITA scores as cheap, biased measurements and sparse MLLM judgments as accurate, costly ones, unifying them via co-kriging, a multi-fidelity Gaussian process, to calibrate relevance across all frames. A temporal determinantal point process then selects frames capturing relevance peaks while preserving temporal coverage. Across Video-MME, MLVU and LongVideoBench, ProbFaS is the most accurate among fourteen state-of-the-art training-free selectors and uniform sampling in 13 of 15 settings (8-128 frames), outperforming the strongest baseline by up to 3.1 percentage points. On MLVU and LongVideoBench, 32 ProbFaS frames match or surpass the top baseline at 128 frames and uniform sampling with up to 768 frames.

**摘要翻译：** 使用多模态大语言模型 (MLLM) 处理长视频的成本极高，因此信息丰富的帧选择对于准确的视频理解至关重要。默认的统一采样经常会错过与任务相关的关键帧，而最近的免训练选择器则根据图像文本对齐（ITA）分数下每个帧与问题的匹配程度来选择帧。然而，我们发现 ITA 信号与 MLLM 的真实需求不太相符：它们的排名与下游准确度相关性较弱，甚至与超过 40% 的问题呈负相关，而 MLLM 的内部是/否判断会产生更强的信号。我们提出了 ProbFaS，这是一种无需训练和无需标签的概率管道，可以融合两个信号以进行关键帧选择。 ProbFaS 将密集的 ITA 分数视为廉价的、有偏差的测量，将稀疏的 MLLM 判断视为准确的、昂贵的测量，通过联合克里金法（一种多保真高斯过程）将它们统一起来，以校准所有帧的相关性。然后，时间行列点过程选择捕获相关性峰值的帧，同时保留时间覆盖范围。在 Video-MME、MLVU 和 LongVideoBench 中，ProbFaS 是 14 个最先进的免训练选择器中最准确的，并且在 15 个设置中的 13 个设置（8-128 帧）中进行均匀采样，其性能比最强的基线高出高达 3.1 个百分点。在 MLVU 和 LongVideoBench 上，32 个 ProbFaS 帧匹配或超过 128 帧的顶部基线，并且均匀采样高达 768 帧。

### Stranger Things: When Objects Appear Without Their Typical Neighbors

**中文标题：** 怪奇物语：当物体在没有典型邻居的情况下出现时

**摘要：** Visual context is a powerful cue for object recognition: scenes inform what, where, and at what scale objects are likely to appear. Yet over-reliance on context becomes a failure mode when models predict objects based on their typical neighbors rather than the objects' own visual features, which we call typicality bias. While extensively studied in the pre-Transformer era, it has often been assumed that architectural and scale improvements would naturally solve it, and a systematic diagnostic across the modern vision stack remains absent. We provide such a diagnostic framework. We measure typicality bias via normalized pointwise mutual information (NPMI) over object pairs, and introduce STRANGE-Bench (Synthetic Typicality-RANked Grounded Evaluation Benchmark): a photorealistic, layout-controlled benchmark spanning 17 sub-tiers from typical to never-co-occurring object pairs. Across five model families (specialist detectors, open-vocabulary detectors, self-supervised, vision-language, and multimodal large language models) and three recognition tasks (supervised detection, open-vocabulary detection, and classification), the bias is prevalent: gaps of 5-11 p.p. persist when objects appear outside their typical company, and do not consistently shrink with scale. To mitigate it, we turn to data augmentation: existing augmentations are designed for visual diversity, not for breaking co-occurrence shortcuts, and sample from the very distribution that produces typicality bias. We propose Anchor-Atypicality Sampling (AAS), a simple sampling rule for paste-based augmentations that uses the NPMI signal to tilt the training-time co-occurrence distribution toward atypical partners. AAS reduces the typicality gap by up to 1.6 p.p. on natural-image COCO splits and 23.0 p.p. on synthetic STRANGE-Bench across three model families, without sacrificing overall performance.

**摘要翻译：** 视觉上下文是物体识别的有力提示：场景告诉我们物体可能出现的内容、地点和规模。然而，当模型根据其典型邻居而不是对象自身的视觉特征来预测对象时，对上下文的过度依赖成为一种失败模式，我们称之为典型性偏差。虽然在 Transformer 时代之前进行了广泛的研究，但人们通常认为架构和规模的改进自然会解决这个问题，并且仍然缺乏跨现代视觉堆栈的系统诊断。我们提供这样一个诊断框架。我们通过对象对上的归一化逐点互信息 (NPMI) 来测量典型性偏差，并引入 STRANGE-Bench（综合典型性排名接地评估基准）：一种逼真的、布局控制的基准，涵盖从典型到从不同时出现的对象对的 17 个子层。在五个模型系列（专业检测器、开放词汇检测器、自监督、视觉语言和多模态大语言模型）和三个识别任务（监督检测、开放词汇检测和分类）中，偏差很普遍：5-11 p.p 的差距。当物体出现在其典型公司之外时，它会持续存在，并且不会随着规模的变化而不断缩小。为了缓解这种情况，我们转向数据增强：现有的增强是为了视觉多样性而设计的，而不是为了打破共现捷径，并从产生典型性偏差的分布中进行采样。我们提出了锚定非典型性采样（AAS），这是一种用于基于粘贴的增强的简单采样规则，它使用 NPMI 信号使训练时间共现分布向非典型伙伴倾斜。 AAS 将典型性差距减少达 1.6 p.p.自然图像 COCO 分割和 23.0 p.p.在三个模型系列的综合 STRANGE-Bench 上进行测试，而不牺牲整体性能。

### Put the World Back: Timestamps Matter in Time Series Reasoning

**中文标题：** 让世界回归：时间戳在时间序列推理中很重要

**摘要：** Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly applied to time series reasoning. However, current evaluations often give models events in chronological order and already matched to the time series. This makes it unclear whether models use timestamps to connect events with observations or simply take shortcuts. To expose this ambiguity, we reformulate time series reasoning from a world-to-series projection perspective, viewing timestamps as explicit indices that connect numerical observations and external events to shared world states. We identify three structures that timestamps can make explicit: temporal causal structure, time semantics, and event-series alignment. Building on this perspective, we develop a diagnostic protocol that distinguishes timestamp-grounded reasoning from correlational shortcuts, a distinction that accuracy-based benchmarks cannot make. Controlled interventions on 310 questions from two tasks show that high accuracy can persist when timestamps are replaced by relative offsets or removed, while breaking event-series alignment induces systematic failures. When context is pre-aligned, models barely need timestamps. When order is missing, they use dates to recall world knowledge. Without dates, they rely on correlational shortcuts and rarely notice incorrect timestamps. These findings show that high accuracy on pre-aligned, ordered inputs does not establish that a model uses timestamps to synchronize observations with the world. We release our code, model outputs, and trajectory labels at https://github.com/tingrui-huang/timestamps-matter.

**摘要翻译：** 大型语言模型（LLM）越来越多地应用于时间序列推理。然而，当前的评估通常按时间顺序给出模型事件，并且已经与时间序列匹配。这使得我们不清楚模型是使用时间戳将事件与观察结果联系起来，还是只是采取捷径。为了揭示这种歧义，我们从世界到序列投影的角度重新制定时间序列推理，将时间戳视为将数值观察和外部事件与共享世界状态连接起来的明确索引。我们确定了时间戳可以明确的三种结构：时间因果结构、时间语义和事件序列对齐。基于这个观点，我们开发了一种诊断协议，可以区分基于时间戳的推理和相关快捷方式，这是基于准确性的基准无法做出的区分。对两项任务中 310 个问题的受控干预表明，当时间戳被相对偏移取代或删除时，高精度可以持续存在，而破坏事件序列对齐会导致系统故障。当上下文预先对齐时，模型几乎不需要时间戳。当顺序缺失时，他们会使用日期来回忆世界知识。如果没有日期，他们就依赖相关快捷方式，很少注意到不正确的时间戳。这些发现表明，预对齐、有序输入的高精度并不能证明模型使用时间戳来将观察结果与世界同步。我们在 https://github.com/tingrui-huang/timestamps-matter 发布了代码、模型输出和轨迹标签。

### Knowledge-Base Gradient Descent: Self-Structuring Learning through Question Answering

**中文标题：** 知识库梯度下降：通过问答进行自我结构化学习

**摘要：** We consider the problem of constructing knowledge bases for applications based on large language models, in which the structure of a knowledge base determines how an application can retrieve and connect the information needed to answer a question. We study Self-Structuring Learning, a setting in which questions and expected answers guide the construction of a persistent knowledge base whose editable structure can be a graph schema or the organization of linked knowledge documents. We propose Knowledge-Base Gradient Descent, which diagnoses discrepancies between expected answers and responses obtained through the current knowledge base. At each round, the method proposes a structural edit, rebuilds the knowledge base, and adopts the edit when the learning loss decreases. The retained structure is evaluated on other questions about the same sources. By randomizing over candidate edits, we express the loss change of an edit as a derivative with respect to the probability of selecting it, and we evaluate joint edits as single candidates because their changes interact. Schema Descent instantiates the method for graph schemas. In experiments on large documents, each a bundle of research papers, the updated knowledge cards and graph schemas obtain higher mean Answer F1 on held-out generated questions than their initial structures, with schema gains in every document. On human questions, mean F1 rises for schemas and falls for cards: the updated structures serve the kind of question they were learned from.

**摘要翻译：** 我们考虑为基于大型语言模型的应用程序构建知识库的问题，其中知识库的结构决定应用程序如何检索和连接回答问题所需的信息。我们研究自结构化学习，在这种环境中，问题和预期答案指导持久知识库的构建，其可编辑结构可以是图形模式或链接知识文档的组织。我们提出知识库梯度下降，它可以诊断预期答案与通过当前知识库获得的响应之间的差异。在每一轮中，该方法都会提出结构编辑，重建知识库，并在学习损失减少时采用编辑。保留的结构根据有关相同来源的其他问题进行评估。通过对候选编辑进行随机化，我们将编辑的损失变化表示为相对于选择它的概率的导数，并且我们将联合编辑评估为单个候选，因为它们的变化相互作用。模式下降实例化了图模式的方法。在大型文档（每个文档都是一组研究论文）的实验中，更新的知识卡和图形模式在保留的生成问题上获得比其初始结构更高的平均答案 F1，并且每个文档中都有模式增益。在人类问题上，平均 F1 因图式而上升，而因卡片而下降：更新的结构服务于它们所学到的问题类型。

### Proverbs As A Window On Collective Prescriptions

**中文标题：** 谚语是集体处方的窗口

**摘要：** Proverbs, as repositories of traditional wisdom, have informed human reasoning for millennia. Yet the collective prescriptions they encode have remained largely unquantified. We introduce a computational framework to analyze nearly 50,000 English-language proverbs. Using large language models, validated against human judgments, we map the topical landscape of proverbs, assess their prescriptive power, and quantify their recommendations on key dimensions of human judgment and behavior. We find that most proverbs are prescriptive, offering guidance in domains under individual control, such as preparation and prudent action, whereas proverbs about uncontrollable circumstances, such as fate and mortality, tend to describe rather than prescribe. When prescriptive proverbs take a consistent stance, they favor caution over risk, long-term over short-term outcomes, honesty over deception, and kindness over malice, and show a moderate tendency toward suspicion over trust. On dimensions involving genuine trade-offs, especially autonomy versus compliance, proverbs provide contradicting advice rather than a single recommendation. This analysis provides a quantitative map of the collective prescriptions in a culture's proverbial wisdom, revealing a core of prudent, honest, and forward-looking guidance alongside varied advice where circumstances dictate the best course of action.

**摘要翻译：** 谚语作为传统智慧的宝库，数千年来一直影响着人类的推理。然而，它们所编码的集体处方在很大程度上仍然是未量化的。我们引入了一个计算框架来分析近 50,000 条英语谚语。使用大型语言模型，根据人类判断进行验证，我们绘制了谚语的主题图景，评估它们的规定能力，并量化它们对人类判断和行为的关键维度的建议。我们发现，大多数谚语都是规定性的，在个人控制的领域提供指导，例如准备和谨慎行动，而关于无法控制的情况（例如命运和死亡）的谚语则倾向于描述而不是规定。当规范性谚语采取一致的立场时，它们更倾向于谨慎而不是风险，长期而不是短期结果，诚实而不是欺骗，善良而不是恶意，并且表现出怀疑而不是信任的温和倾向。在涉及真正权衡的维度上，尤其是自主权与合规性之间，谚语提供了相互矛盾的建议，而不是单一的建议。这项分析提供了一种文化谚语智慧中集体处方的定量地图，揭示了审慎、诚实和前瞻性指导的核心，以及根据情况决定最佳行动方针的各种建议。

### Repairing the Trustworthiness of Distilled Language Models

**中文标题：** 修复蒸馏语言模型的可信度

**摘要：** Knowledge distillation enables the deployment of large language models through smaller student models, but the compression process can introduce regressions in trustworthiness even when downstream performance is preserved. Repairing such failures is challenging because the original distillation corpus is often unavailable, while direct preference optimization may alter capabilities inherited from the teacher. We propose a targeted repair framework based on the proximal Bregman response function (PBRF) that addresses both limitations. We estimate the curvature required for PBRF using a surrogate proxy dataset, removing dependence on the original transfer corpus, and extend the framework to preference-based supervision through an odds-ratio objective. This enables the use of preferred and dispreferred response pairs while constraining changes to the distilled model. Our results show that the proposed approach improves targeted trustworthiness behaviors for both pre-training and post-training models while largely preserving downstream performance, providing a practical alternative to re-distillation or unconstrained preference optimization. Our experiment results show that across different distilled models, the trustworthy regression can be improved by up to 8% with less than 1% decrement in perplexity.

**摘要翻译：** 知识蒸馏可以通过较小的学生模型部署大型语言模型，但即使保留下游性能，压缩过程也会引入可信度回归。修复此类故障具有挑战性，因为原始蒸馏语料库通常不可用，而直接偏好优化可能会改变从教师继承的能力。我们提出了一个基于近端布雷格曼响应函数（PBRF）的有针对性的修复框架，解决了这两个限制。我们使用代理代理数据集估计 PBRF 所需的曲率，消除对原始传输语料库的依赖，并通过优势比目标将框架扩展到基于偏好的监督。这使得能够使用首选和非首选响应对，同时限制对蒸馏模型的更改。我们的结果表明，所提出的方法改善了训练前和训练后模型的目标可信度行为，同时在很大程度上保留了下游性能，为重新蒸馏或无约束偏好优化提供了实用的替代方案。我们的实验结果表明，在不同的蒸馏模型中，可信回归可以提高高达 8%，而困惑度下降不到 1%。

### Routeability Before Routing: Routeability Audit Protocol (RAP) and RouteabilityBench for Audited LLM Model Selection

**中文标题：** 路由前的可路由性：可路由性审核协议 (RAP) 和用于审核 LLM 模型选择的 RouteabilityBench

**摘要：** LLM routing aims to choose, for each prompt, which model in a portfolio is most likely to answer correctly. This only makes sense when the benchmark contains enough prompt-specific variation for different models to be best on different inputs. On coarse-scored LLM benchmarks, however, many models often tie for the best score, and the single best model may already leave little room for improvement. We therefore propose a routeability-first audit, instantiated as RAP and RouteabilityBench, a runnable claim-verification package. The audit is retrospective: it runs once the benchmark-portfolio score matrix exists and before router comparisons are interpreted as general evidence, rather than certifying a portfolio that has not yet been evaluated. Across four benchmarks and 66 LLMs, generic routing enhancements such as larger embedding sets, dimensionality reduction, selective abstention, clustering, and stronger regressors do not consistently outperform the single best model. The clearest positive evidence is concentrated in portfolio-dependent Omni-MATH settings, while GPQA, MMLU-Pro, and IFEval show different ways in which routing can remain hard or predictor-sensitive. Our practical recommendation is that routing gains should be reported together with routeability diagnostics, tied-best analysis, portfolio construction details, and family-corrected statistical tests.

**摘要翻译：** LLM 路由旨在针对每个提示选择投资组合中的哪个模型最有可能正确回答。仅当基准包含足够的特定于提示的变化以使不同模型在不同输入上达到最佳效果时，这才有意义。然而，在粗略评分的 LLM 基准上，许多模型通常都获得最佳分数，而单个最佳模型可能已经没有什么改进的空间了。因此，我们提出可路由性优先的审核，实例化为 RAP 和 RouteabilityBench，这是一个可运行的声明验证包。审计是回顾性的：一旦基准投资组合得分矩阵存在，并且在路由器比较被解释为一般证据之前，它就会运行，而不是认证尚未评估的投资组合。在四个基准和 66 个法学硕士中，通用路由增强功能（例如更大的嵌入集、降维、选择性放弃、聚类和更强的回归器）并不总是优于单一最佳模型。最明显的积极证据集中在投资组合相关的 Omni-MATH 设置中，而 GPQA、MMLU-Pro 和 IFEval 显示了路由可以保持困难或预测敏感的不同方式。我们的实际建议是，布线增益应与可布线性诊断、最佳捆绑分析、投资组合构建细节和族校正统计测试一起报告。
